Command line

CommandWhat it does
battery-health-centerOpen the window.
battery-health-center --backgroundStart hidden and keep recording history (this is what the login item runs).
battery-health-center --export-report FILE.htmlWrite the battery report and exit.
battery-health-center --export-csv FOLDERWrite the four CSV files into the folder and exit.

Files and services

Path or unitPurpose
/usr/bin/battery-health-centerThe app.
bhc-helperThe only part that runs as root. It validates its arguments and writes the charge-limit files and its own configuration. Started through PolicyKit.
/etc/battery-health-center.confThe saved charge limit that Keep After Restart re-applies.
/etc/battery-health-center.scheduleThe saved ready-by times and lead time.
/var/lib/battery-health-center/unplug-logThe unplug times learned by optimized charging.
battery-health-center-restore.serviceRe-applies your limit at startup and after sleep.
battery-health-center-scheduler.service and .pathRun the schedule; the .path unit starts the service only while a schedule exists.
/sys/class/power_supply/BAT*Where the battery readings come from (read directly; no root needed).

The exact configuration file locations can differ slightly between packages; the How it works notes describe what each piece does.

Glossary

Capacity (maximum capacity)
How much energy the battery holds when full now, compared with when it was new.
Design capacity
What the manufacturer rated the battery for when new, in Wh.
Cycle count
The number of full charge-and-discharge equivalents. Two half charges make one cycle.
Wh (watt-hour)
A unit of stored energy. A 50 Wh battery can power a 10 W laptop for about five hours.
Charge threshold
A firmware setting for where charging stops (end threshold) and resumes (start threshold).
Power profile
The system's performance setting: Power Saver, Balanced or Performance.
Energy impact
An estimate of how much battery an app uses, based on its CPU time.
Sleep drain
Battery used while the laptop is suspended, as percent and percent per hour.
s2idle, S3 and S1
Sleep states. s2idle (Modern Standby) wakes quickly, S3 (deep) uses the least power, S1 is a light sleep.
Calibration
A full charge, a full discharge and a full charge that lets the battery gauge relearn the real capacity.
PolicyKit
The standard Linux service that asks for your password before an app makes an administrator-level change.
