What's new

By Alexis Rios-Negron · Battery Health Center 0.6.4

This page lists what changed in every release and links to the full explanation of each feature. Your version is shown in the About Battery Health Center dialog (main menu).

Version 0.6.4#

November 8, 2026

  • Help button and user manual. A question-mark button at the top of the sidebar, F1 and User Manual in the main menu open this manual in your browser. See Finding your way around.

Version 0.6.3#

November 8, 2026

  • About dialog fits without scrolling. It is more compact, and its Voltkit link now goes to the Voltkit source page, where the README is.

Version 0.6.2#

November 7, 2026

  • More Linux Apps. The About dialog links to Voltkit and ThermalPrint, with their official icons.

Version 0.6.1#

November 6, 2026

  • Screen reader support. The charge ring and the charts are named and described (the latest value and what the colors mean), level bars announce their value, icon-only buttons have names and decorative icons are hidden. See Accessibility.
  • Clearer charts. Chart colors keep at least 3:1 contrast on light and dark cards, and periods on the charger get a strip along the bottom of the chart so they do not rely on color alone. The dark theme's accent keeps 4.5:1 with white button text. See Battery level and power.

Version 0.6.0#

November 4, 2026

  • French and Simplified Chinese. The app is now available in English, Spanish, German, French and Simplified Chinese, following your system language.
  • Translated documentation. The README and How it works are available in Spanish, German, French and Simplified Chinese.

Version 0.5.1#

November 3, 2026

  • Cleaner sidebar. The duplicate app name row at the top of the sidebar is gone.

Version 0.5.0#

November 3, 2026

  • A refreshed app. New navigation with a sidebar, adaptive workspaces, a modern battery icon and a branded About dialog. See Finding your way around.
  • Dashboard columns. Overview, Health and Usage use two columns in a large window, while Charging and Power use one centered column so settings stay in reading order. The columns stack in a narrow window.
  • Charging status at a glance. Cards show the active charge limit, the next scheduled full charge and any temporary override. See Charging status.
  • Clearer energy numbers. The app explains how the app energy estimates work, and empty charts say what to do. See Apps using the most energy.
  • Failed saves are reported. A banner names the file and the error, preferences are kept if saving fails, and health and app-usage history are retried once storage recovers. See Where your data is stored.

Version 0.4.5#

November 1, 2026

  • Links in About. The About dialog links to the project website and the issue tracker.

Version 0.4.4#

November 1, 2026

  • First-launch question. The first time you open the app it asks whether to record history in the background, so history is not lost when the window is closed. The answer is remembered, and people who already had settings are not asked. See Your first launch.

Version 0.4.3#

October 28, 2026

  • Updates reach the hidden copy. The background copy started at login used to keep running the old version after an update. Now, once the program file has been replaced and no window is open, it starts the new version and exits. See Record history in the background.

Version 0.4.2#

October 28, 2026

  • Housekeeping. Code that only existed for the discontinued snap package was removed. Nothing changes for the apt and Fedora packages.

Version 0.4.1#

October 27, 2026

  • Clearer labels. Current Mode on the Power page, Notify At in Preferences, and Charging Behavior with a While Plugged In row on the Charging page. See Charging behavior.

Version 0.4.0#

October 27, 2026

  • Charge profiles. Maximum Lifespan (60%), Balanced (80%) and Full Capacity (100%). See Charge limit.
  • Charging schedule and optimized charging. The limit is lifted shortly before the times you choose, or before the times learned from when you usually unplug, and put back afterwards, without asking for a password each time. See Charging schedule.
  • App detail. Click an app on the Usage page to see its energy impact and estimated energy use hour by hour over a day or a week. See App detail.
  • CSV export. Export Data as CSV in the menu and the --export-csv option. See Export data as CSV.
  • Smarter recommendations. While on battery, the Overview suggests turning down a bright screen, turning off the keyboard backlight, or turning off Bluetooth when nothing is connected. See Recommendations.

Version 0.3.3#

October 26, 2026

  • Spanish and German. The app follows the system language and shows English for anything not yet translated. The application menu entry and its description are translated too.

Version 0.3.2#

October 26, 2026

  • Crash fix. Changing Screen Blank or Automatic Suspend no longer crashes the app.
  • Fits the ring. The charge ring says "Not Charging" instead of the wider "Plugged In, Not Charging".

Version 0.3.1#

October 26, 2026

  • Power Mode fix. Choosing a mode in the dropdown is no longer undone a moment later.

Version 0.3.0#

October 26, 2026

  • The Power page. Power mode, automatic power mode, GNOME's power-saving settings, and the power button and lid actions. See Power.
  • Debian and Ubuntu packages. Charge limits ask for a password through PolicyKit, and a system service re-applies a saved limit at boot and after sleep. See Install.

Every release is also listed, with its download, on the releases page.
