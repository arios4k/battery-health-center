Battery Health Center User Manual

Everything about your laptop battery, in one place. Health, cycle count, charge limits and schedules, and which apps drain it, for GNOME. This manual explains every feature, with screenshots.

A quick tour

  • Know how healthy your battery is. Maximum capacity against design capacity, condition, cycle count, a daily trend with the date it will reach 80%, and a guided calibration.
  • Make it last for years. One-tap profiles (60%, 80% or 100%), a custom limit that survives restarts and sleep, and a one-time full charge for a trip.
  • Full when you need it. Tell it when you need the laptop, or let optimized charging learn your routine.
  • See what is draining it. The battery level and power for the last day or week, sleep drain, and apps ranked by energy.
  • Power modes without the hunt. Power Saver, Balanced or Performance, switched automatically when you plug in, plus GNOME's power-saving settings.
  • Take your data with you. An HTML battery report and CSV exports, from the menu or the command line.

Install in one minute

Ubuntu, Debian and derivatives, from the signed apt repository, and Fedora from COPR. See Getting started for the commands.

Help inside the app

Press F1, click the question-mark button at the top of the sidebar, or choose User Manual from the main menu to open this manual from Battery Health Center.
